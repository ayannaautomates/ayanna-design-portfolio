Survey creation and metrics

Vendor Needs Survey

A survey I designed for a private client’s vendor community, with the results turned into a KPI dashboard that shows what needs the most help.

12Vendor responses
82%Struggle to find opportunities
5Call late payment a major challenge

Open-ended answers asked for shorter certification requirements, protection from slow payment by general contractors, and lower proposal costs for small firms.

The survey has 31 questions, including a construction-only section. Each challenge is scored on a four-point scale and ranked by the share of vendors who rated it major or moderate.
